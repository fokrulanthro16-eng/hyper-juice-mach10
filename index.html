Mathematical & Programmatic Architecture

1. Dynamic Focal Length Warp

To produce an authentic hypersonic sensation, the camera lens contracts non-linearly from an intimate 50mm to an ultra-wide 18mm FOV:

$$\text{Lens}(f) = 50.0 - 32.0 \cdot \left(\frac{f - 60}{25}\right)^{1.5} \quad (60 \le f \le 85)$$

Followed by high-speed optical breathing ($\pm 0.6\text{mm}$) during sustained Mach-10 cruise.

2. Mach-10 Exponential Velocity Curve

Forward motion across the 2.2km tunnel is governed by an exponential power curve rather than flat linear velocity:

$$Y_{\text{cam}}(f) = Y_0 + L_{\text{tunnel}} \cdot \left(\frac{f - 60}{156}\right)^{2.35}$$

Instantaneous velocity reaches >33.3 m/frame (approx. 2,880 km/h) at the exit portal, producing intense sub-frame motion blur streaks.

3. Multi-Harmonic Perlin Camera Shake

Deterministic closed-form Fourier harmonics generate organic cockpit buffeting without external dependencies:

$$\text{Shake}_X(t) = A(f) \cdot [0.22 \sin(1.73t) + 0.11 \sin(3.41t)]$$ $$\text{Shake}_Z(t) = A(f) \cdot [0.18 \cos(1.91t) + 0.09 \cos(4.13t)]$$

The envelope $A(f)$ ramps at surge detonation and smoothly dampens to 0.015 in open space.

4. Procedural Geometry & Shaders

Every object is constructed via from_pydata() with clean quad topology and Voronoi micro-plating:

$$\theta_{\text{twist}}(i) = i \times 0.092 \text{ rad} \quad (\text{Helical Vortex})$$ $$\text{Quad Topology: } 940\text{ Objects } \mid 931\text{ Meshes}$$

Real-time Compositor Fog Glow glare (threshold 0.70), and chromatic dispersion (0.012).
